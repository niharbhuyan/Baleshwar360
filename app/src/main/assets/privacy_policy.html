1. Introduction & Overview

Nihar Sales ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to respecting and protecting the privacy rights of all users of the Baleshwar 360 mobile application. This Privacy Policy details our transparent policies regarding the collection, use, on-device processing, and disclosure of data when you utilize our community utilities, coastal safety features, and offline tourism guides.

2. Data Minimization & Offline-First Design

Baleshwar 360 is engineered as an offline-first resilience utility. The core features of the app—including offline vector maps, cyclone emergency guides, bus timetables, artisan directories, and historical tidal curves—function fully on your device without requiring user account creation, sign-in, or transmission of personal information.

3. Information We Collect & Purpose Specification

a. Location Information (Foreground Only)

Permissions: ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION

  • Purpose: Calculating distance to the nearest Multipurpose Cyclone Shelter (MCS), displaying nearest hospital emergency blood banks, and determining the safe-walk recession corridor on Chandipur Beach.
  • Safeguards: Location is requested in the foreground only. We do not track your location in the background when the app is closed, and we do not sell or share GPS coordinates with advertisers.

b. Camera Access (ML Kit QR Code Scanner)

Permission: CAMERA

  • Purpose: Allows on-device scanning of official Balasore tourism posters, festival guides, and municipal notices.
  • Safeguards: Processing is performed transiently in memory using Google ML Kit. No photos or video recordings are saved to storage or transmitted over the network.

c. Microphone Access (Voice Search)

Permission: RECORD_AUDIO

  • Purpose: Powers optional voice-to-text searching in English and Odia.
  • Safeguards: Audio snippets are processed ephemerally on-device or via system speech recognition and immediately discarded.

d. Emergency Calling & SMS Dispatch

Permissions: CALL_PHONE, SEND_SMS

  • Purpose: Facilitates one-tap calling to district emergency helplines (112, 1077, 108) and dispatch of emergency SOS messages during natural disasters.
  • Safeguards: Actions require explicit, manual user confirmation and cannot be triggered in the background.

e. Zero-Permission Media Selection

We use the Android system Photo Picker (ActivityResultContracts.PickVisualMedia) for voluntary review photos. We do not request or access broad device storage.

f. Battery Health Telemetry

Android BatteryManager is queried locally on-device to throttle background WorkManager sync tasks below 20% battery. Battery metrics are never uploaded to remote servers.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We partner with reputable industry-standard SDKs that comply with Google Play Developer Policies:

  • Google AdMob: Serves community ads with non-personalized configurations and disabled advertising ID collection. AdMob Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Delivers critical push alerts for IMD cyclone warnings and flood gate notices.
  • Firebase Analytics: Aggregates anonymous screen views and usage telemetry to improve app performance.

5. Data Retention & User Deletion Rights (14-Day Commitment)

Under Google Play's User Data Policy, you maintain complete rights over your data:

  • In-App Cache Purge: You can purge all cached news, maps, and offline tables via Settings → Local Cache Management → Purge Cache.
  • Account & Content Deletion: To request permanent deletion of your account, feedback, or any associated data, email niharbhuyan@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request". We will process and verify your request within 14 calendar days.

6. Children's Privacy

Baleshwar 360 is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal details, please contact us for immediate deletion.

7. Security Safeguards

All network communications are secured using industry-standard TLS 1.3 / HTTPS encryption. Local data is secured within the sandboxed Android application directory.
