Privacy Policy

Application: Baleshwar 360 • Package ID: com.niharsales.baleshwar360 • Effective Date: November 17, 2026 • Developer: Nihar Bhuyan

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the Baleshwar 360 mobile application developed by Nihar Bhuyan (niharbhuyan@gmail.com). This policy describes how we handle information in compliance with Google Play Store Developer Policies, GDPR, and Indian Information Technology laws.

2. Information Collection and Usage

Baleshwar 360 is built with a privacy-first, offline-capable architecture:

  • Optional Account Sign-In: Core features work without an account. Users who choose to sign in with Google can sync bookmarks, preferences, and Citizen Civic Eye reports across devices via Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore.
  • Local Storage (Room Database): Saved bookmarks, preferred language settings (Odia / English), and offline news/weather entries are cached locally on your device storage.
  • Location Usage: Foreground location is requested only when tagging Citizen Civic Eye infrastructure reports or triggering an emergency SOS alert.

3. Device Permissions & Justifications

The application declares only standard permissions necessary for its primary utility purposes:

  • android.permission.INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Enables downloading real-time weather telemetries, tidal tables, river flood levels, and news feeds.
  • android.permission.CAMERA: Used only when capturing photos for Citizen Civic Eye infrastructure reports.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION: Used when geotagging civic issues or sharing emergency SOS coordinates.

4. Advertising & Third-Party SDKs (Google AdMob)

Baleshwar 360 integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) (App ID: ca-app-pub-4880243637225183~4742987253) to serve advertisements that help maintain and update the application free of charge.

Google AdMob and its partners may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID), IP addresses, and diagnostic information to serve ads and prevent fraud in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

Users can opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting their Android device settings: Settings > Google > Ads > Reset or Delete Advertising ID.

Read Google's privacy policies here: Google Privacy Policy and Google Ads Privacy.

5. Children's Privacy

Baleshwar 360 does not target or knowingly collect data from children under 13 years of age. If you believe child data has been inadvertently collected, please contact us immediately.

6. Data Security and Deletion

Users retain complete autonomy over their data. You can delete synced cloud data from the User Profile sheet or request account data deletion by emailing niharbhuyan@gmail.com. Clearing application data or uninstalling the app permanently purges all local caches and settings.
